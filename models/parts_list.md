# MyRobot 3D Print Parts List
## Ryan Beck
### 9/29/2026


All 3D print files designed in [onshape](https://www.onshape.com/en/).

## Files
Model files are divided into 3 sections:
* Chasis
* Drive Train
* Electronics

The models in each section, along with their required quantities, are listed below.

**Chasis**
*Print one of each item in the chasis.*
* Back Door
* Body Lid
* Body
* Head Lid
* Head
* Weight Holder

**Drive Train**
* 2x Drive Gear Washer
* 2x Drive Gear 
* 4x Intermediate Axle
* 4x Intermediate Gear Washer
* 4x Intermediate Gear
* 8x Snap Ring
* 4x Wheel Axle
* 2x Wheel Gear Left
* 2x Wheel Gear Right
* 4x Wheel Gear Washer

**Electronics**
*Print one of each item in the electronics.*
* Battery Casing
* Battery Clip

## Print Settings
I printed all of the parts with the following general settings:
* PLA (specifically esun PLA+ that I got on sale)
* 0.16mm layer height
* 2 wall loops (not required, but could bump to 3 for added weight and strength)
* 15% sparse infill
* All parts are modeled to not require supports (Axle ends may be questionable, but as long as the clip will go in it's fine.)

I've also included my OrcaSlicer project will all the required parts ready in 3 separate build plates.
